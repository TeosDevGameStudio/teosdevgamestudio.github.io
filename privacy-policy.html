Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

The T Path is a 3D endless runner game. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is used when you play our game.

Information We Collect

The T Path does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts or photos

However, the app uses advertising services that may automatically collect limited, non-personal information.

Advertising

The T Path displays ads using third-party advertising services, including Unity LevelPlay (ironSource and Unity Ads).

These services may collect and process the following information:

  • Device identifiers such as the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), when permission is granted
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and performance data)

This data is used to:

  • Display ads within the app
  • Measure ad performance
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on user consent

On iOS, the app may request permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT). If permission is denied, ads may still be shown but will not be personalized.

In-App Purchases

The game includes optional in-app purchases handled securely by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store any payment or billing information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party SDKs for advertising, including:

  • Unity LevelPlay
  • ironSource
  • Unity Ads

These services may collect limited data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

The T Path is suitable for all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads shown to children may be contextual or non-personalized.

Your Choices

You can control ad tracking permissions at any time from your iOS device settings.
