Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

The T Path is a simple 3D endless runner game. We take privacy seriously and want to be transparent about what information our app collects.

Information We Collect

The T Path does NOT collect any personal data.

  • No name, email, phone, or address
  • No location data
  • No online identifiers
  • No gameplay analytics
  • No tracking across apps or websites

In-App Purchases

The game includes optional in-app purchases handled securely by the App Store. We do not see or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

The T Path does not use ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Children’s Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from children.
